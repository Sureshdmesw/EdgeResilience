## Snapdragon / HP Positioning

EdgeResilience is designed for deployment on **Snapdragon-powered HP PCs**, using Snapdragon X Elite-class edge compute and Qualcomm AI Hub / QNN optimization for AI inference acceleration.

Current hardware evidence was generated on a **Snapdragon X Elite CRD through Qualcomm AI Hub**.

Physical measurement on a specific HP Snapdragon-powered PC is not claimed.
